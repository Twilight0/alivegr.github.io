Projects, profiles and contact channels keep multiplying — AliveGR here, AliveOS there, GitHub, YouTube, Telegram, email… The linktree fixes that: one short URL with the essentials (this site, AliveOS, Ko-fi support) plus every currently available social: **Email, YouTube, Facebook, GitHub, X, Google Play Store, Patreon and Telegram**. Bookmark that instead of hunting links across bios.
