**MEGA Player** is now available for Kodi — this is its first release.

* Source: [github.com/Twilight0/plugin.video.megatv.com](https://github.com/Twilight0/plugin.video.megatv.com)
* Install via the [Twilight0 Repository](https://github.com/Twilight0/repository.twilight0) → Video add-ons → MEGA Player

## Where the idea came from

The concept originated in 2020, during the residential confinement of the lockdowns. I had time I had never had before, and a provider I watched almost daily.

## A project that was never finished

Back around 2016, in a discussion with the renowned **Lambda** developer, it was indicated that an add-on for this provider would not be implemented — the reasoning being that the station was being gradually discontinued. The expectation was reasonable; MEGA was heading the same direction as most of Greek television at the time.

What happened instead was stranger: the provider went quiet for a period, cut staff, changed management — and came back with its original content fully restored.

So this add-on is, in a sense, the project that developer never got to complete.

## The family of add-ons

Other add-ons — **Ant1 Player, SKAI, Star and Alpha** — were originally his creations as well. On his retirement I took over maintaining them, with his knowledge of my continuation of the work. I expect he watches with some amusement how the Kodi team handles situations involving "banned add-ons," in the sense of the [official list](https://kodi.wiki/view/Official:Forum_rules/Banned_add-ons).

## A note on what this software does

This add-on and my repository are included in that category of work, so to be clear about the nature of it:

My software does not infringe upon any intellectual property rights. It retrieves original content directly from the provider's own websites. In certain instances, content is mirrored to cyberlockers, which is considered a permissible practice.

## Status

First release, fresh code, things will need polishing — feedback, edge cases and bug reports are welcome in [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/Twilight0/plugin.video.alivegr/discussions).
