## About

Twilight0 develops open-source Kodi addons focused on Greek content. Everything below is distributed and updated through the [Twilight0 Repository](https://github.com/Twilight0/repository.twilight0) for Kodi 20 (Nexus) and 21 (Omega). Three components form the core of the work:

### 📺 AliveGR

The flagship video addon — one addon for everything Greek: live TV, movies, series, sports, music, radio, news, kids and documentaries, aggregated from freely available public sources. Nothing is hosted; it acts strictly as a directory and search client.

* Source: [github.com/Twilight0/plugin.video.alivegr](https://github.com/Twilight0/plugin.video.alivegr)
* Install: [Twilight0 Repository](https://github.com/Twilight0/repository.twilight0) → Video add-ons → AliveGR (see the [Install & FAQ](/en/install/) guide)

### 🔌 PluginsGR

The resolver pack that powers Greek playback: dedicated resolvers for official Greek broadcast portals, complementing ResolveURL so streams extract fast and directly, without proxy overhead.

* Source: [github.com/Twilight0/script.module.resolveurl.pluginsgr](https://github.com/Twilight0/script.module.resolveurl.pluginsgr)
* Installed automatically as an AliveGR dependency via the repository

### 💬 Subtitles.gr

The dedicated Greek subtitles provider for Kodi (v4.0.0): Subs4Free, GreekSubs.net, YIFI, TVsubtitles.net and Moviesubtitles.org, with IMDb-ID matching — plus a global context-menu companion (`context.subtitles.gr`) for one-click subtitle download from any library item.

* Source: [github.com/Twilight0/service.subtitles.subtitles.gr](https://github.com/Twilight0/service.subtitles.subtitles.gr) · Companion: [github.com/Twilight0/context.subtitles.gr](https://github.com/Twilight0/context.subtitles.gr)
