Your one addon for everything Greek

AliveGR (stylized exactly like that) is a Kodi addon for streaming content available mostly in Greek. Live TV, radio, news and newspaper front pages, movies, TV series and shows, sports, music, kids content, documentaries, short films and more — scraped from freely available public sources across the web. The addon itself hosts nothing; it acts as a search engine over public domains.

Source Code · Install via Repository

Main Features

Live TV ▶
Greek live TV channels, plus networks, guide data and godmode listings for non-stop viewing.
Movies ▶
On-demand movies with Greek subtitles or audio, resolved via ResolveURL and PluginsGR helpers.
TV Series & Shows ▶
Greek and foreign series, TV shows and theater performances in one place.
Sports ▶
Sports video on demand — highlights and replays to watch on your schedule.
Music & Radio ▶
Music content plus radio stations via installable companion addons.
Kids ▶
Cartoons, kids series, kids live channels and kids songs — safe browsing for the little ones.
News ▶
News streams and high-quality newspaper front pages, updated daily.
Documentaries & Educational ▶
Documentaries and educational content for curious minds of all ages.
Customization & Operability ▶
History, bookmarks, switchable themes, resolver plugins and full Kodi community support — on any platform.
